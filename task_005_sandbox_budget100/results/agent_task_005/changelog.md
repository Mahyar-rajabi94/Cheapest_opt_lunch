(2) Inside the trust region the latent LogEI collapsed monotonically from −7.4 (eval 63) to −25.1 (eval 70), with 10/10 trust-region evaluations failing to beat −11.35533. The GP refit on all 70 points attributes noise sd ≈ 0.152 (y units) to this roughness, while the latent mean near the incumbent is about −11.1 to −11.2. Latent EI therefore compares a smooth mean against one "lucky" plateau value and declares improvement impossible. EI on the observation-level predictive distribution accounts for the plateau-to-plateau scatter the data actually show. (4) I didn't use the pre-registered second-basin restart because a model-only diagnostic (20k allowed samples, no evaluations) found the best distinct non-incumbent basins at posterior mean −10.77, −10.63 and −10.60 (sd ≈ 0.10–0.11). The global observation-level EI still lets those basins win if the model rates them highly.

## Evaluation 80
**Change:** Stopped "logei_obs_global" after 10 evaluations (71–80). The remaining 20 evaluations (81–100) go to "compass_local", a Hooke–Jeeves-style coordinate pattern search centred on the best allowed point (eval 43, y = −11.35533). Probes change one coordinate at a time: step 0.04 for x0, x2, x3, x5, x6, x7 and 0.10 for x8. x0 moves are coupled with an x2 decrease to stay on constraint 5, which is active at eval 43 (slack 1e-6). x9 and x10 are switched to an alternative level. Each probe's sign is the one with the lower GP posterior mean among the allowed options. On improvement the centre moves and the same displacement is repeated once (pattern move). A probe that returns a value identical to the centre's (same plateau) gets its step doubled in the next sweep. A probe that is worse gets the opposite sign in the next sweep.
**Reason:** (1) The 0.02 exclusion radius was smaller than the plateaus. Evals 71, 72, 73 and 75 returned the identical value −11.25391 although they span 0.063 in x8, 0.0125 in x6 and 0.010 in x2. Evals 79 and 80 returned the identical −11.29748 while differing by 0.0216 in x6. So 4 of the 10 evaluations re-measured already-known plateaus. (2) Evals 71–80 stayed between −11.254 and −11.297 (no improvement on −11.35533), and LogEI_obs declined only slowly (−4.83 → −4.26). The model's trend minimum sits around −11.24, and the observation-level EI keeps sampling neighbouring cells of that one flat region. (3) The structure around the incumbent is non-smooth. A linear trend fitted to the 39 cluster points (x1 ≈ x4 ≈ 0, x10 = 0, y < −10.4) has R² = 0.51 and residual sd 0.187, and eval 43 is the largest outlier (residual −0.349, next −0.191). Multi-coordinate moves of only 0.011–0.055 away from eval 43 made y worse by +0.32 (eval 70) and +0.51 (eval 67), even when they followed the fitted trend direction. A smooth GP cannot resolve which coordinate causes these jumps. One-coordinate-at-a-time pattern search, with steps above the observed plateau widths, is a derivative-free method suited to piecewise-constant objectives and isolates each coordinate's effect around the best point.
