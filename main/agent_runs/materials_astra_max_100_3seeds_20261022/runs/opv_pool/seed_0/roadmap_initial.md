# Initial decision: one bounded diagnostic design

The public task is minimization over 11 coordinates in [0,1], with no additional constraints and a hard total budget of 100 objective calls. There are no prior observations. The objective is noise-free but history-dependent: the same coordinate vector can return a different value after other evaluations. Therefore observations are outcomes along this campaign trajectory, and a previously best point has no guaranteed repeatable value. No objective identity or private implementation will be inspected.

For this first block, use a scrambled Sobol design plus a repeated reference: evaluate the cube center (0.5 in all coordinates), evaluate 16 Sobol points in sequence, then evaluate the center again. The reference pair measures one realized history effect; it does not establish a universal time trend or random-noise distribution. This modest diagnostic cost is justified by the explicit nonrepeatability.

Implementation: provided src.optimizers.sobol_search.SobolSearch, backed by SciPy 1.17.1 scipy.stats.qmc.Sobol, with d=11, scramble=True, seed=0, and bounds [0,1]^11. The 16 design points use a complete power-of-two block for Sobol balance. Inherited SciPy defaults include bits=None (30 effective bits) and optimization=None; NumPy is 2.4.6. No optimizer fit or prior objective observations initialize this design. The active optimizer label is sobol_reference. All stochastic choices use campaign seed 0; there are no derived seeds in this step.

Maximum additional objective calls: 18. Pause at cumulative evaluation 18. Sixteen broad points provide an initial view of spatial variation without spending a large part of the budget; two reference observations provide a direct check of the history effect. No later method or budget allocation is selected yet.

At the checkpoint, inspect all observed values, the best point, range and quantiles of the 16 design outcomes, reference change relative to that range, spatial proximity of good points, and possible order effects. History and spatial effects may be confounded, so avoid treating these data as repeated measurements of a fixed function. Pause immediately on any evaluator error or nonfinite result; do not retry an unknown evaluation. The driver enforces the step cap and total cap before every evaluate() and returns control at the checkpoint. Save through the same harness after the block.
