# Initial decision: one CMA-ES block

The objective is a sealed, deterministic scalar black box to minimize in 10 real coordinates, each in [-5, 5], with no further constraints and no prior observations. The hard campaign budget is 2,000 objective calls, including any repeated points or numerical probes. The optimum and objective identity are unknown and will not be investigated. No smoothness or convexity is assumed.

Use the installed pycma 4.5.0 implementation directly so the population and initialization are explicit. Initialize the mean at the box midpoint (all zeros), sigma at 2.5 (one quarter of the box width), population size 16, and random seed 20261022. Use full covariance adaptation (CMA_diagonal=0), active covariance updates (CMA_active=True), and pycma's bounds transformation for [-5,5]. Recombination weights, learning rates, damping, and termination tolerances inherit pycma 4.5.0 defaults. Suppress console and file logging with verbose=-9 and verb_log=0. The midpoint gives a neutral starting location; the broad initial distribution and moderately enlarged population support exploration and covariance learning without assuming derivatives or a specific landscape.

The first step allows at most 160 additional objective evaluations, pausing at cumulative evaluation 160: ten complete generations of 16. Ten generations allow initial adaptation and a comparison of early and late populations while using only 8% of the budget. There is no commitment to another method or block after this step.

At the checkpoint inspect best observed value and its location, generation minima and medians, objective range, sigma, covariance axis ratio, coordinate sampling scales, and any native termination flag. Assess whether progress and concentration support continued search; a short plateau is not conclusive. Pause sooner for a native optimizer stopping condition; stop immediately and report an evaluator error or a nonfinite result, without retrying an unknown evaluation. Every call is guarded by the step and total limits, and all results come exclusively from the same harness.evaluate method. Preserve the live optimizer and harness across checkpoints and call harness.save at each checkpoint.
