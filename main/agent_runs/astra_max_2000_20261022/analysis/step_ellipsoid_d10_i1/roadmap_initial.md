# First optimization step

Minimize the deterministic scalar black box over 10 coordinates, each in [-5, 5], with no additional constraints and no prior observations. The hard total budget is 2,000 objective calls. The objective identity and optimum remain unknown. Determinism is supplied by the public interface; continuity, smoothness, and unimodality are not assumed to be established.

Use bounded CMA-ES through cma.CMAEvolutionStrategy from pycma 4.5.0, via a campaign-local driver in work/campaign.py. The provided CMA wrapper fixes population and scale; the supported underlying API permits explicit settings. Initialize mean x0 = [0]*10, sigma0 = 2.5, population size = 14, seed = 20261022, and bounds = [-5,5] in every coordinate. Set verbose=-9 and verb_log=0 to suppress incidental logs. Retain pycma 4.5.0 defaults: active full covariance updates, default log recombination weights (7 positive parents), cumulative step-size adaptation, BoundTransform box handling, rank-one/rank-mu learning rates, tolx=1e-11, tolfun=1e-11, tolfunhist=1e-12, and tolstagnation=325 generations. Do not override ordinary internal adaptation. Inspect optimizer stop conditions at generation boundaries.

This method is a derivative-free starting choice for an unknown 10-dimensional response: it can adapt directions and scale without assuming coordinate separability. The midpoint avoids privileging a corner, the initial scale is one quarter of the width, and 14 samples per generation balance exploratory coverage and feedback frequency. No observations exist yet to support a landscape claim.

The first block permits at most 196 objective calls (14 full generations), then pauses at cumulative evaluation 196. Fourteen generations provide multiple covariance and step-size updates while leaving most of the budget uncommitted. Every sample passes through the same harness.evaluate() and through a local block guard. No future block or method is selected here.

At the checkpoint inspect running-best and generation-median progress, objective spread, mean location, adapted scale, axis ratio, and evaluations remaining. An optimizer stop condition pauses the block early. Any evaluator error or nonfinite objective halts the campaign without retrying an unknown evaluation. A brief plateau alone is not evidence of failure. Call harness.save() at the checkpoint and retain the live optimizer and harness for the next evidence-based decision.
