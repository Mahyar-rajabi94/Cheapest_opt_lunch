# Initial decision: bounded local model trial

The public task is to minimize a deterministic scalar black box in 30 real coordinates, each bounded by [-5, 5], with no additional constraints and no prior observations. The hard campaign budget is 2,000 calls, including initialization and any repeated points. The objective identity and optimum are unknown and will not be inspected. Determinism is supplied metadata; smoothness is only a working hypothesis to test, not an observed property.

Use Py-BOBYQA 1.5.0 from the center x0 = (0, ..., 0), tagged `bobyqa_local`. A campaign-local queue adapter preserves the solver and its interpolation model while the foreground alone calls the same AgentTaskHarness. The equal coordinate scales allow physical coordinates with scaling_within_bounds=False. Set npt=61 (2d+1), rhobeg=1.0 (10% of the box width), rhoend=1e-8, objfun_has_noise=False, seek_global_minimum=False, and restarts.use_restarts=False. Disable logging and progress printing. Set maxfun=2000 as an additional solver cap; the foreground enforces the smaller current block cap and the harness total cap before every evaluation. Leave interpolation updates, trust-region acceptance/radius updates, and other unspecified settings at Py-BOBYQA 1.5.0 defaults. NumPy is 2.4.6; SciPy is 1.17.1. No random initialization or stochastic restart is used.

A local quadratic interpolation method is evaluation-efficient for a deterministic moderately high-dimensional objective if it is locally regular. Its 61 initial samples probe both directions on each coordinate; the remaining calls permit model-directed steps and model repair. The central start leaves room for steps in every direction without assuming where the optimum lies.

Execute at most 180 additional objective calls; pause at cumulative evaluation 180. This gives the 61-point initial model plus 119 adaptation calls, enough to assess descent without committing much of the full budget. Pause earlier if the solver returns, a nonfinite value is observed, an evaluator error occurs (never retry an unknown evaluation), or a numerical/transport exception prevents safe execution. A short plateau alone does not trigger a switch.

At the checkpoint inspect the initial and best values, the number and sizes of improvements, initial coordinate responses, distances moved, boundary proximity, recent progress, and any solver termination message. These observations will determine the next decision. No later method or budget allocation is selected now. Save through the harness at the checkpoint.
