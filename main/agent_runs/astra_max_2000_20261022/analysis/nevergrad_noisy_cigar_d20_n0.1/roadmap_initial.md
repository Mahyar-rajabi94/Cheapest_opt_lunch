# Initial decision: one bounded block

Minimize the noisy scalar objective on 20 coordinates, each in [-5, 5], with no other constraints and no prior observations. The hard total budget is 2,000 calls, including repeats. The public interface says scoring uses the noise-free objective at the point with the smallest observed value; that true value is unavailable to the agent. Noise magnitude and distribution are unknown. Repeats will provide a local diagnostic, not an assumption of uniform noise everywhere. The objective is treated as a sealed black box.

The first block uses 8 independent calls at the box center x = 0, followed by 12 complete CMA-ES generations of 16 candidates (192 calls). Method labels are `cma_noise_probe` for repeats and `cma_es` for the population search. CMA-ES begins at x = 0 with sigma0 = 2.0 (20% of the box width), identity covariance, full covariance adaptation (`CMA_diagonal=0`), active covariance updates (`CMA_active=True`), population size 16, and reproducible random seed 593904. Bounds are enforced by pycma's default BoundTransform. Use the supported `cma.CMAEvolutionStrategy` ask/tell API in installed pycma 4.5.0, with its inherited default recombination weights, learning rates, step-size adaptation, and termination rules. Logging/console verbosity is disabled (`verb_log=0`, `verbose=-9`). NumPy is 2.4.6; SciPy is 1.17.1. The supplied CMA wrapper was reviewed; the direct API exposes population size and initial scale for this campaign without a new dependency.

A rank-based population method is suitable for an initially unknown noisy landscape and can learn interactions among 20 variables. The initial scale samples a substantial interior region without concentrating on boundaries. A population of 16 modestly increases selection diversity relative to the dimensional default. The 8 center repeats measure noise against the search's between-candidate variation. They are preserved as observations but do not enter CMA's population updates.

Maximum additional calls: **200**. Pause at cumulative evaluation **200**, or sooner if pycma signals termination, any observation is nonfinite, or the evaluator raises an error (no retry). Eight repeats give a useful preliminary variance estimate; 12 generations allow initial directional adaptation while limiting delayed reconsideration to 10% of the budget. A campaign-local evaluation guard enforces this cap and the global remaining counter. The same harness and optimizer process remain alive at the checkpoint.

At the checkpoint inspect center repeat mean, standard deviation and range; population minima and medians across generations; best observed value and its location; sigma and covariance axis ratio; and calls used/remaining. Judge improvement relative to estimated noise rather than interpreting one low observation as a reliable objective value. Reconsider method, population size, sampling scale, replication and next block length from those observations only. No subsequent block is preselected.
