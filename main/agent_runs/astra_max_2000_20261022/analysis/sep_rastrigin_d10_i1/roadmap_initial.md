# First decision: bounded CMA-ES exploration

The public task is to minimize a deterministic scalar black box in 10 real coordinates, each in [-5, 5], with no other constraints and no prior observations. The total hard budget is 2,000 objective evaluations, including all initialization and repeated calls. The identity, implementation, and optimum remain private. Smoothness, separability, and the number of basins are unknown; this step assumes none of them as facts.

Use the installed pycma 4.5.0 implementation through its supported `CMAEvolutionStrategy` ask/tell API. The provided wrapper fixes some settings, so a small campaign-local adapter exposes population size and initial scale. Initialize the mean at the box center (all zeros), sigma at 2.0 (20% of the coordinate range), population size at 16, random seed at 4401, active covariance adaptation enabled, and bounds at [-5, 5] in every coordinate. Inherit pycma 4.5.0 defaults for rank weights, learning rates, full covariance adaptation, boundary transformation, and stopping tolerances. Suppress library logs (`verbose=-9`, `verb_log=0`). First evaluate the center as a reference; it is recorded but does not update the sampling distribution. Then evaluate 10 complete generations.

The maximum first-step allowance is 161 objective calls: one center evaluation plus 10 generations of 16 candidates. Pause at cumulative evaluation 161. This is long enough to observe initial scale/covariance adaptation and directional progress while consuming only about 8% of the budget. CMA-ES is useful here because gradients and landscape regularity are unknown, and it can adapt to coordinate interactions.

At the checkpoint inspect the center value, best and median values by generation, running-best improvements, sample dispersion, step size, covariance axis ratio, and proximity to boundaries. All reported objective values must come from `h.evaluate`. There is no replication because the interface explicitly states determinism. Pause sooner if pycma reports a stopping criterion at a generation boundary. Stop on any evaluator error without retry, or on nonfinite output/invalid candidate. A short plateau is evidence to assess, not proof that the method is unsuitable.

The single persistent runner retains the same harness, observations, and optimizer state. A guard checks both the current block allowance and remaining campaign budget before every call. Every call is tagged `cma_es`. Save through the harness at the checkpoint. Only this first step is selected; subsequent choices depend on the observations returned to the agent.
