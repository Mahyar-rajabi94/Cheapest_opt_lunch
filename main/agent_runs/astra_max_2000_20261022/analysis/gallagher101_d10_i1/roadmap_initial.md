# Initial decision: bounded CMA-ES block

The public task is minimization of a deterministic scalar black box in 10 real coordinates, each in [-5, 5], with no additional constraints and no prior observations. The hard campaign budget is 2,000 objective calls. Smoothness, separability, multimodality and the optimum are unknown. I assume only the stated determinism; no identity inference or private-source inspection is used.

Use CMA-ES through the installed pycma 4.5.0 supported ask/tell API. The provided wrapper was inspected, but the underlying API permits an explicit population size. Initialize the mean at the box center (all zeros), isotropic standard deviation sigma0=2.5 (one quarter of box width), population size 16, seed 20261022, active covariance adaptation enabled, and full covariance from the start (CMA_diagonal=0). Enforce [-5,5] through pycma's bounds/BoundTransform handling. Other adaptation and termination parameters inherit pycma 4.5.0 defaults; logging and verbose output are disabled. NumPy is 2.4.6. Evaluate the center once as an observed reference, then run ten complete generations, updating the distribution after each generation. The center observation is retained in campaign history but is not inserted into a CMA generation.

This derivative-free, rank-based method is suitable without assuming smoothness or known objective scale. A population of 16 provides broader selection than the dimension-based default of 10 while retaining ten adaptation cycles in a modest initial block. The initial distribution spans a substantial part of the box without requiring observations from a separate design.

**Step limit:** at most 161 additional objective calls; pause at cumulative evaluation 161 (one center plus 10 x 16 candidates). Ten generations allow a first assessment of movement and scale adaptation while preserving most of the budget for evidence-based decisions. The runner checks the block and total allowance before every call, and a generation starts only when it fits.

At the checkpoint inspect the center value, best observed value and location, generation minima/medians, distribution step size, covariance axis ratio, and boundary proximity. Determine whether the population is producing sustained improvement and whether its spread is appropriate; there is no preselected following method. Pause sooner if a pycma termination condition is reached at a generation boundary, a nonfinite value occurs, or the evaluator reports an error. On evaluator error, stop without retrying the unknown call. Save the harness output at the checkpoint. Preserve the live harness and optimizer state between blocks.
