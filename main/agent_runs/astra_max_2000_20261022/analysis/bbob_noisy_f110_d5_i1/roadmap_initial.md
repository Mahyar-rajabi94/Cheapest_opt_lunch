# Initial decision: broad coverage and noise measurement

Minimize a noisy scalar objective in five coordinates, each in [-5, 5], with no other constraints and a hard total budget of 2,000 real evaluations. There are no prior observations. Scoring uses the noise-free objective at the point with the lowest observed y. The objective remains sealed. Noise distribution, magnitude, independence and spatial variation are unknown; repeated measurements will give preliminary, not definitive, estimates. No smoothness or separability is assumed as a fact.

The first step is `sobol_noise`: 64 scrambled Sobol points covering the full box, followed by eight additional evaluations at each of four distinct anchors: the two smallest initial observations, the initial observation at median rank, and the remaining design point closest to the box center. Each anchor therefore has nine observations including its design observation. Including median and central anchors helps avoid estimating noise only at selected low observations.

Implementation: provided `src.optimizers.sobol_search.SobolSearch`, backed by SciPy 1.17.1 qmc.Sobol, dimension 5, scramble=True, seed=20261022. SciPy defaults are inherited for bits (30) and optimization (None). Bounds are the public box. No optimizer model is initialized and there are no additional method hyperparameters. A campaign-local persistent driver retains the sole harness, all actual observations, and method state. It guards every objective call against both block and total limits. Only harness.evaluate produces objective observations.

Maximum additional evaluations: 96. Pause at cumulative evaluation 96. Sixty-four is a balanced power-of-two Sobol design; 32 repeat calls give four small within-point noise samples while using only 4.8% of the total budget. This step is long enough to compare broad variation with noise, but short enough to reconsider the search method early.

At the checkpoint inspect objective quantiles, locations of low observations, replicated means and standard deviations, consistency of the two initially best points, and noise scale relative to the spatial variation. A nonfinite result or evaluator error ends the block immediately; no unknown evaluation is retried. Otherwise complete this bounded design and return control for a fresh decision. No later method or allocation is selected in advance.
