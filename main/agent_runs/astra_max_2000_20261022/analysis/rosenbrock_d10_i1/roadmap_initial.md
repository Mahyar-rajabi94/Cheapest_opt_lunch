# First decision: bounded CMA-ES exploration

Minimize a deterministic scalar objective in 10 real coordinates, each bounded by [-5, 5], with no additional constraints. The hard campaign budget is 2,000 objective calls, including initialization and any repeated points. There are no prior observations. The objective remains a sealed black box; no smoothness, separability, optimum value, or landscape identity is assumed. No noise replication is needed under the public deterministic specification.

Use the installed pycma 4.5.0 `CMAEvolutionStrategy` directly, because the provided wrapper fixes population size. All coordinates are normalized to [0, 1] before optimization and transformed back for the harness. Initialize the distribution at the midpoint (0.5 in every normalized coordinate), with isotropic sigma0 = 0.25, population size 16, seed 104729, active covariance updates enabled, and pycma's bounded-coordinate transform. Inherit pycma 4.5.0 defaults for recombination weights, covariance and step-size adaptation, and stopping tolerances; disable console and disk optimizer logging (`verbose=-9`, `verb_log=0`). This is a rank-based method that can adapt correlated directions without assuming gradients. Population 16 gives a moderately broad sample in 10 dimensions; sigma0 covers a meaningful fraction of the box without choosing a preferred region.

Evaluate the midpoint once as an observed reference, then at most 10 complete CMA-ES generations. Maximum additional calls: 161. Pause by cumulative evaluation 161. The reference point is retained in campaign observations but is not inserted into a synthetic CMA population. Ten generations give initial evidence on improvement, distribution contraction, and objective spread, while using only about 8% of the budget. This is only the first block; no subsequent method or allocation is preselected.

At the checkpoint inspect the best observed value and point, improvement across generations, generation medians and ranges, normalized step size, covariance axis ratio, and any optimizer stopping condition. Pause earlier at a complete generation if pycma reports a stop condition. A short plateau alone will not establish convergence. On an evaluator error or nonfinite value, stop immediately without retrying an unknown evaluation. The controller checks the block and campaign counters before every real evaluation. All objective values come only from `h.evaluate`; use the same live harness and optimizer across checkpoints, and save harness records after the block.
